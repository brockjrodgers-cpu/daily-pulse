📅 1-Click Lock-Screen Calendar Reminders

Mobile browsers pause web tabs when your phone is locked. Download a recurring .ics Calendar file for your chosen daily reminder times (09:00, 13:00, 17:00) so iOS/Android gives you reliable lock-screen push notifications even when Safari or Chrome is closed:

🍎 iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with upward arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Daily Pulse from your Home Screen and tap Tap to Enable at the top for push alerts.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Tap to Enable in the app header to allow system notifications.
